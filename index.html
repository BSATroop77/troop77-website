Welcome

Every boy deserves the chance to discover what he’s made of: to paddle a river at sunrise, reach the top of a trail he wasn’t sure he could finish, and sit around a campfire with friends who have his back.

At Troop 77, adventure is how we grow. Our Scouts fly planes, run whitewater, mountain bike 22 miles, climb & rappel, canoe & kayak, camp under the stars and serve their community. Along the way they learn to plan the trip, lead the patrol, and look out for each other.

Leadership comes from being trusted with something real. If he’s ready to try, we’re ready to welcome him. Visit us any Monday night and start the adventure.
