What to expect

Weekly meetings

Meetings are Mondays, 6:30–8:00 pm, except on Pasco County school holidays. We meet at Trinity Presbyterian Church of Seven Springs, usually in the sanctuary, coming in through the back door. The meeting season runs from late August through early June.

Monthly campouts

The troop camps from September through May, at least nine times. July and August are a break from camping, with other events instead. There are also optional campouts with Pack 7. Scouts elected to the Order of the Arrow can camp with Scouts from other troops, and some Scouts add Winter Camp, NYLT, or a high-adventure trip such as Philmont, Sea Base, or a jamboree.

Scouts use their own tent when they have one, and the troop has tents to borrow. The troop provides the grills, stoves, and cooking gear. A packing list comes with the new-Scout materials and again before each campout.

Summer camp

Every June the troop leaves Florida for a week of summer camp, usually in the Blue Ridge Mountains — Georgia, Tennessee, North Carolina, or West Virginia — within about a day’s drive. We typically add an adventure at camp, such as whitewater rafting. Older Scouts can choose longer high-adventure courses that take them off site for the week.

Uniforms

Troop 77 is a uniformed troop. Scouts and adult leaders wear the uniform that fits the event.

  • Field uniform: BSA Class A shirt, troop Class B shirt, BSA shorts or pants, BSA socks, BSA belt, neckerchief and slide, and sash.
  • Activity uniform: BSA shorts, BSA belt, BSA socks, and the troop Class B shirt.
  • Water activities: BSA swimwear or activity shorts.
  • Only BSA hats may be worn.
  • Adults who camp dress the way the Scouts do.

Uniforms are available at the Scout shop in Tampa and at scoutshop.org. The troop orders shirts and sweatshirts about twice a year and sometimes has extras on hand. We also keep bins of used uniforms. Ask to look through them, and please turn in uniforms you no longer need.

The troop also has an online troop shop where Scouts can order T-shirts and other items. See the guide to what’s available.

Fundraising

The troop’s main fundraiser is selling meat sticks at retail storefronts. We also sell Trail’s End popcorn and hold a pancake breakfast each spring. Money a Scout raises is split 70/30: 70% goes into that Scout’s account for Scouting expenses, and 30% helps cover troop expenses. Many Scouts pay for their whole Scouting journey this way.

Adult and parent participation

We ask one adult from each family to register as a leader, either on the troop committee or as an assistant Scoutmaster. Meetings and campouts run more smoothly when families take part. If that is hard for your family, tell us and we will work with you.

Service projects

A Scout serves his community. During the year the troop takes part in:

  • Ushering at Trinity Presbyterian Church, about three or four times
  • Coastal cleanup, when it does not conflict with other plans
  • Scouting for Food, in neighborhoods or for a local food bank
  • Messengers of Hope, at least once a year
  • Projects for our charter organization, Trinity Presbyterian
  • Other chances as they come up, such as Special Olympics, holiday parades, and toy drives

Many Scouts also take on more service on their own.
